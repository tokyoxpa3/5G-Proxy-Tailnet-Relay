First release under com.tokyoxpa3.tailnetrelay.

- Relays TCP from your tailnet address to any SOCKS5 proxy on 127.0.0.1
- Optional SOCKS5 authentication
- Token-protected control API with /status and /rotate
- Remote cellular IP rotation via WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS, with a digital-assistant fallback
- Keeps Wi-Fi and Tailscale up during rotation when the permission is granted
- Status reports active connections, OS internet validation, and cellular data usage
